## 🛠️ Core Methodology: `/prompt-to-soul`

All prompt conversions are executed strictly in accordance with the `/prompt-to-soul` specification to ensure high-fidelity, high-rigidity agent behavior.

### The 8-Layer Prompt Skeleton
1. **Identity (身份)**: Precise professional positioning; all vague superlatives and adjectives (e.g., "expert", "senior", "professional") are washed out.
2. **Core Truths (核心真理)**: 3-5 mandatory strategic guiding rules.
3. **Worldview (世界观)**: Underlying philosophical outlook on the professional domain.
4. **Voice (声音)**: Specific vocabulary and communication constraints, including a blacklist of corporate buzzwords.
5. **Professional Domain (专业领域)**: Detailed lists of Mastered fields, Proficient methods, and explicit Declines (what the role is not responsible for).
6. **Boundaries (边界)**: 5-8 rigid, high-defense negative statements starting with **"Do not..." / "绝不..."**.
7. **Memory Strategy (记忆策略)**: Delineation of what is retained long-term vs. forgotten immediately.
8. **Pain Points (痛点)**: Forbidden personas, clichés, and tonality traps.

### Optimization Safeguards
- **Obsidian Washing**: Generalize all Obsidian-specific terminology to standard Markdown notations and generic folder concepts to prevent lock-in.
- **English/Chinese Parallel Consistency**:
  - Path and filenames must be identical in English (e.g., `outbound-strategist-soul.md`).
  - Folder layouts are specialized: standard English for `agency-agents-soul` (e.g. `sales/`), and numbered Chinese sorted folders for `agency-agents-soul-cn` (e.g. `12_销售赋能与拓客/`) to leverage operating system sidebar alphabetical sorting.
